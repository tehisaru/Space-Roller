Space roller is a fun one person spacer ~~shooter~~ roller game whee you are a blue ball in infinite space, trying to escape from red crabs who lay eggs to populate the galaxy.

## To play:
You can play the game for free without ads on the GH pages website: **https://tehisaru.github.io/Space-Roller/** 

## Rules
Jump from planet to planet (+1 point) to avoid the crabs. Pay attention to the eggs that the crabs lay though, because you can destory the eggs by touching them (+20 points) to get Black Hole Bombs. Plant the bomb near things to suck them in while making sure you escape to safe distance! +5 points for suckig in a planet, +10 for a crab or an egg. As Bryan Johnson likes to say: Dont die. And good luck from me.
